Photosensitivity warning

Nothing moves until you press Start. At its defaults Bubble doesn't strobe: one film drains for about 30 seconds, bursts slowly and is pulled again a second later. Some settings do flash, faster than three times a second, and flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. Measured on Resolume's own demo clips: Burst at 0 with Refill under 0.1 s and Slow Motion below its default (up to 11 flashes a second over part of the frame); Pop pressed twice a second or faster with a short Refill (up to 6.5 a second); and, at the defaults, a clip that already flickers, which Exposure brightens (2 of Resolume's 33 demo clips).

This page's generated clips don't flicker; a video of your own can. The line under the picture warns when the settings pull and burst a film faster than three times a second. Pause, under the picture, stands the film still at once.
